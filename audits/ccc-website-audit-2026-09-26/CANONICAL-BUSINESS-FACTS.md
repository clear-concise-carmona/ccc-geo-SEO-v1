## Rules applied

- A fact is APPROVED_FOR_DRAFT_USE only when (a) the published wording is consistent across every observed first-party surface, (b) no competing version was found, and (c) the fact is not a quantitative outcome, client name, or credential count that still needs a source or permission.
- Owner-authored strategy documents (EV-032 to EV-035) count as evidence of intended positioning and offer structure. They do not substantiate outcomes or statistics. Where they disagree with the live site, the live site is treated as current and the disagreement is listed for decision.
- "Live" means read from the fetched HTML on 2026-09-27 (EV-038, EV-039, EV-043). "Index" means the 2026-09-26 search-result sample, now known to lag the site. "Canon" means the page the CCC Source-of-Truth Map (177930242) names as canonical for that domain, read through the Atlassian connector on 2026-09-27 (EV-045). Per the Map, Confluence wins unless the page is outdated, a newer approved source exists, or Jeremy overrides it; where canon and the live site disagree, the live copy is treated as drift and the fix is a copy edit the owner approves.
- HOLD facts must not appear in publication-ready files. They may appear in drafts labeled APPROVAL_REQUIRED.
